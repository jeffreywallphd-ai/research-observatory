An alternative owner choice requires a revised exact packet and review; this proposal does not assume approval.

It substitutes authenticated amendment authority for the two forbidden task fields only for the named intentional amendment task. Existing ordinary `1.0` and resumed-restoration `1.1` lanes retain their behavior and denials. No generic filename, status label, or self-asserted contract grants admission.
