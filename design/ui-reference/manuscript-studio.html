4. Results

AI support changed creative quality through repeated-cycle learning

Table 3 reports the pre-registered multilevel models. The immediate main effect of AI support on expert-rated creative quality was positive but imprecisely estimated. [12 verified result and method sources]

The interaction between AI support and project cycle was positive (β = .21, SE = .08, p = .009), indicating that supported teams improved more across repeated cycles than comparison teams.

The treatment-by-cycle pattern was robust to alternative outcome codings and attrition bounds. The preregistered cognitive-load outcome did not differ reliably between conditions. [4 verified report anchors]

Teams with lower initial expertise benefited most from AI support. Blocked: exploratory analysis and inconsistent population
