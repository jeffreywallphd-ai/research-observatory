# ADR-0041: Authenticate reviewed activation control repairs

## Context

The actual integrated A03 check rejected six authenticated W2 planning-page
changes that reverted to their original bytes. Its immutable T02 manifest
correctly contains 118 net paths. Per-commit history and the endpoint inventory
are separate proofs; neither may hide intermediate product or control changes.
The official accessibility checker also omits standard explicit label
associations recognized by the pinned browser. Existing adverse results stay
preserved. Accepted ADR-0001/0003/0004 remain authoritative.

The owner approved the exact ECR-0013 packet at `55c6389c` and its separate
B00 source addendum. The completed bootstrap, whole paused A03 snapshot at
`771e54a3`, adopted A04 and original full `6506c684` task base are fixed inputs.

## Candidates

1. Replace frozen manifests, exempt generated filenames or shorten task bases.
   Reject because these choices could conceal source or invent authority.
2. Keep the actual adverse results and A03/W2 paused. This remains the recovery
   state if the bounded repair cannot satisfy the approved contract.
3. Authenticate the exact separate reviewed segment under existing closed v1.4,
   with a distinct default-empty inert-output map and accurate label checking.
   Select within the owner-approved two-task A05 envelope.

## Decision

T01 authenticates the six exact regular Git rows from the immutable preflight
through reviewed A04/A03 delivery. The generic reviewed-task check retains the
net inventory, overlap denial and every unreviewed add/revert denial. No path,
prefix or caller declaration confers an exemption.

Authenticate the exact four preceding GOV27 commits directly through the
owner-approved maintenance binding, preserving prepared-first bytes, adverse
R01 and approved R02. Admit the subsequent two B00 source blobs only through
their separate immutable proposal, patch, owner addendum and actual B00 review.
No old maintenance helper, imported tool, controller, schema or revision changes.

Both A05 tasks need distinct claims and independent committed dispositions.
Keep their five-path and four-path source envelopes separate from A03/A04.
One Proposed ADR41 and one appended registry entry satisfy ADR-0001. T02 may
append only its implementation/verification body while retaining all metadata.
All prior ADR bytes and registry entries remain unchanged.

Integrated S01, exit and a separate committed security review precede adoption.
Return the complete still-paused A03 record with both tasks DONE. A later
separate A03 activation does not reopen either task. Fresh A03 review-02 must
replay and explicitly close its retained S01 finding before its own adoption.
The original task's two contracts, reference/witness/baseline, current
independent classification and capture input closure remain mandatory.

T02 recognizes only same-document standard explicit label associations and
preserves wrapping labels, existing naming order, duplicate-ID/drift checks
and actual unnamed-control failures. It changes no approved markup, pixels,
baseline or reference and grants no native/Core qualification.

## Consequences

This companion documents the already approved bounded delivery and supersedes
no accepted decision. Security, privacy, rights, research I/O, migrations,
deployment profiles and release authority remain unchanged. On failure retain
the paused holds, immutable candidates and adverse findings; remediate only
within A05 scope. No remote effects or baseline rollback are authorized.

## Verification

Use actual retained-history attribution and hostile real-Git projection,
hidden-source, predecessor, review, source-mode and return/activation tests.
Run affected quality, ADR/UI authority and canonical planning checks at the
exact committed candidate, followed by expanded independent review.
Future disposable records establish control behavior only.

T02 additionally needs focused static/browser name positives and negatives and
the fresh unmodified official 33-page/66-state accessibility command. Actual
A05 adoption, A03 integrated finding replay and adoption, product-native/Core
checks and fresh full W2 qualification remain separate later evidence.
