## Intended delta and invariants

- Current guidance, templates and new packet generation use rolling-wave
  planning. Future scope is provisional; the complete upcoming Wave is detailed
  and approved once. Accepted architectural authority still requires its normal
  successor/approval route; unrestricted planning is not unrestricted execution.
- New `initiation-assessment-2.0` packets permit substantial planned refactoring
  and major redesign. Exact final upcoming-Wave task coverage, finite estimates,
  units, identities, allocation portions and review duties remain required.
- Future decision incompleteness does not block a decision-complete current
  contribution, but current blockers still do. The legacy capability-approval
  shortcut cannot approve a rolling-wave packet. Only whole-Wave approval applies.
- Keep version 1.0 validation and all frozen W1/ECR approvals and exceptions.
  New approvals cannot select the obsolete policy. No historical migration or
  retroactive backfill is performed.
- The execution cap applies to cumulative supplemental work beyond approved
  scope. Approved planned redesign is not retroactively capped. Past-Wave work,
  mixed work, spent/remaining effort, and all amendments share the original
  denominator. No automatic budget reset, denominator inflation or implied
  exception is authorized.

Execution accounting is **review-enforced** in existing task/amendment evidence.
The existing historical ECR validator checks individual packets, not cumulative
Wave spend; this increment does not claim otherwise or add an unwired controller.
Before additional refactoring, an older active Wave needs evidence-based
reconciliation; missing historical effort is unknown, not free headroom.
This increment implements the requested new operating policy, not product
cleanup or a structural debt-refactoring project.
