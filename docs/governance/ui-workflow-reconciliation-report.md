The Vision, Systems Design, Implementation Plan/backlog, Automation Guide, governance/start document, Academic Minimal style guide, workflow catalog, and linked HTML reference are complementary after the 1.3 expansion. ## 4. Fourteen approved workflows

The eight original literature/research-opportunity workflows remain unchanged in purpose; production critical and hermeneutic support is now completed in W7 so theory/critical article workflows do not depend on a later wave. Six production workflows were added: empirical study design, empirical study to article, empirical results to article, theory article development, critical article development, and manuscript review/revision. `WORKFLOW_CATALOG.json` is the machine-readable authority; the same workflow definitions drive project creation, adaptive navigation, validation, and documentation.

## 5. Page and capability coverage

The approved reference contains **32 product pages plus two reference pages**. Six new product pages realize the expanded scope: Study Design Studio, Manuscript Blueprint, Technical Reports & Results, Manuscript Studio, Reviewer Simulation, and Revision & Response. Every product page maps to approved capabilities and required regions. CAP-12/CAP-13 hosted administration remains intentionally absent until W10/W11.

## 6. New domain and integrity boundaries

- Literature evidence and study-result evidence remain distinct.
- Study-design alternatives require human methodological/ethics approval.
- Unreported results are never inferred.
- Manuscript drafting requires an approved blueprint and validated claim support.
- Reviewer reports are independent before editorial synthesis.
- Simulated review is advisory, does not predict acceptance, and does not impersonate named reviewers.
- Author dispositions, diffs, responses, and re-review remain provenance.
- Technical reports and drafts are private/local by default; remote egress is explicit and auditable.

## 7. Automation consistency

`taskctl` enforces campaign, slice, and task transitions. Verification profiles cover cross-platform desktop, study design, reports/results, manuscripts, reviewers, end-to-end local behavior, and private-content security. Experience automation validates reference approval/hashes, workflow/page/capability contracts, links, shared assets, themes, interaction, accessibility, visual baselines, and application conformance.

## 8. Items deliberately unchanged

The modular-monolith plus workers architecture, Tauri/React desktop, Python/FastAPI core, local project-home model, SQLite/object storage, evidence-first ontology, provider-neutral model gateway, human authority, bounded novelty, rights-aware document handling, Academic Minimal visual direction, and university/cloud deployment concepts remain intact.

## 9. Final consistency statement

The 1.3 documents describe one coherent system: an evidence-first, cross-platform research-development environment that starts from a declared scholarly objective; builds inspectable literature evidence; identifies and challenges opportunities; supports study or argument design; integrates verified results; drafts against an approved article architecture; simulates independent review; governs revision; and preserves complete provenance, privacy, authorship, and human judgment.
