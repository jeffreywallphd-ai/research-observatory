The new runner measures the complete frozen DBLP/ACM qualification corpus and a
202-import-record plus one retained-connector synthetic project. The protected
path uses public batch prepare/schedule, a genuine worker job, DPAPI/SQLCipher,
rights-aware source reads, a new warm request/job, feature-cache reuse, and both
100-item and later candidate pages. A separate nonqualifying smoke completed;

smoke's first and later pages each took about 13.5 seconds on this machine. The
10-second number in the independent preflight was explicitly provisional, not
an approved or measured baseline.

The runner requires three fresh Windows child processes, exact committed source,
test, CSV, JSON, tool and installed-dependency inputs, a fresh bytecode prefix,
fixed hardware and method, every raw sample, finite positive values, complete
workload identity and accuracy, and a separately pinned reviewed baseline.
Calibration cannot report a performance pass. Qualification is denied while the
baseline hash is pending; any failed invocation replaces a stale pass with FAIL.
Every future sample must fit both the independently reviewed calibration maximum
plus 20% and the fixed absolute workload ceiling. The 20-second page ceiling is
proposed here for review in light of the smoke; it is not a passing claim.

The fixture uses synthetic Crossref transport and ASGI/native context. Setup
alone substitutes worker capacity. Measured batch execution does not substitute
worker admission or source authority. OS caches are not flushed. These results
cannot establish live-provider latency, installer performance, native GUI
rendering, minimum-hardware performance, researcher agreement, or error-correction
rates. All private project material and raw logs stay in ignored local artifacts.
