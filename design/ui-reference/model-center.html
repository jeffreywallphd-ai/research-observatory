Actual role evidence is separate from structural validity

No role score or human review has been observed. Proposed custom extraction promotion requires equal-field macro precision≥0.90 and recall≥0.80 on the frozen eligible answerable frame; empty/abstained answers count0, and no consequential failed field is hidden by the macro. Proposed verifier promotion requires actionable-label macro precision≥0.90, separate SUPPORTS and REFUTES recall≥0.80 and answered coverage≥0.80. Undefined denominators or missing gold class cannot pass; technical failures are failures, not abstentions.

These exact rules come from the manifest-bound selected policy03, not official leaderboard equivalence or universal scholarly quality.
