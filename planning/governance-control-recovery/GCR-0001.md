# GCR-0001 — Restore a generation-safe recovery controller

## Decision request

Authorize one exact, independently reviewed root bootstrap that repairs the recovery controller itself while preserving the sole active `HOLD-W1-GRR-0002`. This is a last-resort control-generation request, not an ordinary ECR, a new GRR hold, an amendment, or Wave execution authority.

## Why this lane is necessary

The repository followed the approved GRR-0002 and ECR-0003 paths through an exact W1.A04 packet approval and a tested four-file B00 candidate. Atomic `taskctl amendment bootstrap-submit` then failed because the released GRR-0001 hold validator is not prefix-stable: when future W1.A04/ECR-0003 is appended in memory, the historical GRR-0001 check includes that later ECR and incorrectly demands ECR-0004.

The mandated same-hold supplement is also unavailable. `recoveryctl supplement-start` accepts only control revision 4, records predecessor revision 4, and advances to the current controller revision 6. The live state is already revision 6, and the v1 supplement boundary additionally requires the target amendment and its bootstrap to exist in the backlog even though this defect prevents that append. No existing approved packet authorizes a direct fix, B00 reopening, competing hold, Wave resume, or manual ledger mutation.

The failed append was atomic. W1 remains `PAUSED`, `CAP-02.S04.T03` remains `BLOCKED`, W1.A04 is absent from the backlog, G1 remains pending, and `HOLD-W1-GRR-0002` remains the sole ACTIVE hold.

## Selected design

Introduce a narrowly bounded Governance Control Recovery (`GCR`) root-of-trust lane for the case where all of the following are proven:

1. an existing ACTIVE GRR hold already denies ordinary execution;
2. the approved repair amendment cannot be appended or enforced because of a recovery-controller defect;
3. the same-hold supplement installer cannot represent the current control generation;
4. no existing approval authorizes the correction; and
5. the proposed repair can remain governance/controller/schema/test-only.

GCR-0001 does not create or replace a hold. Its exact packet, independent packet review, and human exact-commit approval authorize only `GCR-0001.B00`. B00 advances the minimum control revision from 6 to 7 under the existing GRR-0002 hold, adds an append-only control-generation record, and makes the ordinary supplement lane capable of representing `GRR-0002.S01/B01` without inventing W1.A04 backlog state.

The new revision must:

- validate released holds against their historical prefix rather than later amendments;
- parameterize supplement predecessor and successor revisions instead of hard-coding revision 4→6;
- preserve v1 supplement history while adding a versioned pre-append repair target for the approved ECR-0003/W1.A04 B00 boundary;
- deny older readers after revision 7 is recorded;
- require one exact append-only GCR implementation submission, independent control/security review, and no open blocking finding before the new supplement controller is used; and
- retain all existing hold, review, hash, path, stale-writer, dirty-tree, identity, and atomicity controls.

## Execution sequence

1. Freeze this inert packet at one Git commit.
2. Obtain independent control/security packet review.
3. Obtain explicit human approval of the exact packet commit.
4. Implement only `GCR-0001.B00` within its path/outcome allowlist on the W1 Codex branch.
5. Bind criterion evidence to the approval-to-candidate diff and obtain independent B00 review.
6. If approved, retain the active GRR-0002 hold and prepare `GRR-0002.S01` under the revision-7 supplement format.
7. Independently review and obtain exact human approval for GRR-0002.S01, install and approve only B01, then retry the original W1.A04 B00 submission.
8. Continue W1.A04 and W1 only through their existing separate gates.

## Safety boundary

GCR-0001 grants no W1.A04 append, amendment materialization, task claim or recovery, T03 mutation, hold release, Wave resume, slice/Wave approval, G1 approval, main integration, remote push, product/reference/privacy change, or review bypass. Any adverse packet or B00 review remains append-only and stops execution.

## Recommendation

Approve the exact reviewed GCR-0001 packet. Keeping W1 stopped is safe but leaves the repository permanently unable to exercise its prescribed supplement lane at revision 6. Direct edits, reopening an approved bootstrap, faking predecessor revision 4, or creating a competing hold would break the authority chain and are rejected.
