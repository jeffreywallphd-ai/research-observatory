# CAP-04.S04.T01 task-start acceptance closure

Claim base: `0cd9e588541ec952a9c6ad6437297828493b5a0d` on the approved W2 campaign. The frozen Wave packet is `c85a59f3a293f8e3f2eaf6454682c9a14b1efa55`. This task models project-scoped CorpusItem status, decision reasons, and immutable discovery paths. It does not implement the T02 rights evaluator or T03 Corpus Canvas/reports.

## Authority and recorded mismatch

The authoritative backlog requires `candidate`, `included`, `excluded`, `pending`, `duplicate`, `unavailable`, and `withdrawn`. The same approved slice plan §4 instead names `discovered` and `awaiting-decision` in place of `duplicate` and `pending`. Preserve all seven backlog conditions: `discovered` is an immutable discovery-path event, `awaiting-decision` describes a pending human review, and duplicate is a recorded relationship to an exact canonical target. This interpretation is subject to independent task review; no named backlog condition may be dropped or silently aliased.

Inherited `domain-lifecycle.v1.json` defines only candidate/included/excluded/withdrawn for `corpus-item`. Its bytes, existing transitions, and historical readers stay intact. `domain-core.schema.json` and the v16 aggregate identity/revision CHECK constraints do not admit `corpus-item`; mapping it to `record` or `decision` to gain persistence would violate the domain lifecycle boundary. A first-class canonical CorpusItem therefore needs a versioned successor authority, a task-linked Accepted ADR and generated compatibility catalog entry, a source-retaining v1 bridge, and additive v17 storage migration. The planned successor must preserve all existing identity, provenance, outbox, dependency, encryption, backup, and recovery guarantees. If that cannot be done within the approved W2 scope, use the mismatch/amendment route before dependent implementation.

The design must not force logically independent conditions into one lossy exclusive value. Membership uses the existing four-state lifecycle; pending review, duplicate relationships, and source/copy availability are separately versioned facts. The public CorpusItem projection must expose all seven named conditions and their concurrent meanings (for example, an included Work may have unavailable full text) without overwriting accepted membership. If the approved task is instead interpreted to demand a single seven-destination lifecycle, that is a further breaking successor decision and must be justified explicitly before use.
