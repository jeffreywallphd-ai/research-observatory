# ADR-0058: Scoped evaluation attestations and honest local coder authority

Unnumbered private proposal, not accepted architecture, a gold approval, a named-human assignment or an evaluation result. There is no reserved ADR ID, release, model eligibility or W3 execution authority.

## Context

[ADR-0002](ADR-0002-govern-the-evaluation-registry-as-a-support-only-repository-area.md) makes `evaluation/` support-only; product cannot import its cases, scripts or mutable approvals. ADR-0021 binds model results to exact route/config/evaluation/source identities. ADR-0025 owns durable execution/human-decision history; ADR-0026 owns the fourteen semantic navigation profiles, not analytical job success. Vision 7/9/12 requires source inspection, missingness, contestation, human interpretation and plural adjudication, not one confidence score or a fluent model rationale.

Refinement-02 selects existing SciFact/QASPER human annotations and a finite mapping bridge rather than an invented annotation study. Root-selected policy03 fixes the non-vacuous extraction/verifier rules before candidate comparison. These choices are incorporated in this complete private successor and remain unaccepted; the proposal is not evidence that models/data/people/rights are ready.

## Candidates

1. Let runtime read support registry files and activate any schema-valid model. Easy to implement but violates ADR-0002 and confuses structural validity with role quality.
2. Keep gold/reports/approval work support-only, import validated immutable evaluation attestations through product commands, and require scoped role promotion plus current rights/resources. Use separate source-first verifier and human tasks with honest local identity limits. This retains portable, inspectable product authority without a hosted service.
3. Require hosted multi-user IAM and cryptographic hostile-owner blinding before local coding, or let model consensus be final adjudication. The former exceeds local W3; the latter contradicts researcher authority. Retain later institutional identity as a future separately designed deployment, never a claim from this local implementation.

## Decision

Propose candidate 2. Runtime prompt/schema/tool assets stay runtime-owned and versioned. Support registry assets retain immutable benchmark frame/gold/mapping/report lineage; no product dependency on `evaluation/`. Product accepts a strict attestation binding exact model/runtime/profile, role/task/domain, prompt/schema/config, source and report digests, metric/method/frame, actual independent disposition, validity and explicit human promotion. Attestation import neither executes evaluation nor adopts a baseline silently. Missing, stale, substituted, unsupported-domain or failed evidence cannot promote an ordinary role. Technical fixture qualification remains distinct from scholarly promotion and uses the first proposed decision's real authority boundary.

Bind the exact `metricPolicy` object of root-selected policy03, SHA256 `1c40fcbe2ed959479e74305986b4b2cc7efed18d2fa7895c0a148cf07dc76d81`; the accompanying manifest references the actual file and independent policy review. Its exact rules are incorporated in this private successor contracts, gold strategy and every named acceptance row, pending genuine incremental review and ordinary authority before the single Wave approval. Do not silently retain the earlier precision-only verifier shorthand.

For the custom QASPER adapter, freeze eligible (question ID, simple extractive field ID) units and whole acceptable human-reference annotations before comparison. Policy03 specifies NFC/newline/casefold tokenization, retained punctuation/duplicate counts, multiset overlap, highest-F1/precision/stable-annotation tie-breaking, answerable abstention/empty output P=R=0 and equal-field macro precision>=0.90/recall>=0.80. Technical failure remains a failed run. Empty gold is not free credit; unsupported/free-form/unanswerable/missing gold and consequential-field failures remain visible. This is not official leaderboard equivalence or universal scientific extraction.

For SciFact verification, retain SUPPORTS, REFUTES, NOT_ENOUGH_EVIDENCE and ABSTAIN separately. On the complete frozen eligible development frame, actionable-label macro precision>=0.90, each SUPPORTS/REFUTES recall>=0.80 and explicit answered coverage>=0.80 are all required. Undefined/zero prediction denominator or absent required gold class cannot pass. Abstention/missing prediction counts missed; technical/invalid/timeout failures cannot be excluded or renamed abstentions. Publish full confusion, actual Ns, coverage/rationale anchors and uncertainty intervals, not only a passing aggregate. Unjudged evidence is not negative gold.

The exact gold strategy retains official splits/annotations/disagreements, release/digest/source notices, deterministic pre-comparison membership and the 30-case human mapping bridge. SciFact publisher/card license discrepancy and QASPER source/revision rights remain genuine acquisition prerequisites; this proposal grants no license or egress permission. Use data-only acquisition, never executable dataset scripts or model-generated replacement gold. Actual competent reviewer/adjudicator identities/results remain absent until supplied. The initial biomedical-claim/NLP-QA domains do not qualify PICO/theory/causal interpretation, other languages, screening-protocol recall or medical decisions. Model pretraining contamination remains unknown unless evidenced.

Extraction writes candidate immutable records with exact revision/anchor/original wording, schema/prompt/model/profile and lineage. Independent deterministic schema/anchor/rights validation precedes acceptance. Verifier input contains the candidate, cited source and schema, not extractor rationale/hidden chain of thought. Separate prompts/packets/roles are mandatory; serial reuse of one model remains correlated. A second model only counts as distinct where actual evaluated and resource-admitted. Neither model owns inclusion, stopping, ethics, interpretation, final claim, export rights or human adjudication.

Confidence decomposes retrieval/source quality/extraction/comparability/interpretation/corpus coverage; unknown is explicit. Not reported, unclear, not applicable and inferred are different from zero/false/observed. High-consequence, inferred, disputed and low-confidence fields receive human review. Audit combines consequence strata and a seeded random frame with actual inclusion probabilities, Ns, outcomes and appropriate interval assumptions; an absent audit or low prediction rate cannot imply validated recall. Human decisions retain actor, role, rationale, exact candidate/source/schema and allowed consequences; alternatives remain immutable.

Local coder names are human-declared research attribution bound to the authenticated Windows application actor and immutable assignment. Enforce assignment and hide peer decisions at the application API until submission under the declared blind condition. The shared project owner/OS administrator may inspect local data; this is neither proof of distinct people nor hostile-owner cryptographic blinding. No hosted identity or physical foreign-host witness is supplied. Adjudication offers explicit consensus, designated resolution or unresolved plurality, preserves originals and binds downstream consequences. Kappa/alpha/agreement report field type, actual N, missingness and assumptions, not truth.

## Consequences

Strict role eligibility can leave every candidate unavailable; manual work remains usable, but a promised model-supported outcome is not silently declared complete. Reusing publisher annotations reduces new labeling work without removing source rights, mapping competence or independent review. More explicit states increase UI/storage work but prevent schema-valid output or correlated agreement from becoming scholarly authority.

Add attestation, assignment, candidate/status, audit and adjudication data through backup-first versioned migration from the actual predecessor. Preserve accepted legacy evidence, raw old status, human decisions and run/attempt/lease identities. Do not retroactively label old outputs evaluated or create human actors. Unknown new versions deny; retain a compatible reader/upcaster or reopen a verified original backup rather than destructively downgrade. Upgrades mark affected role attestations/derivatives stale and require deliberate comparison/promotion; rollback disables the new model/pack and leaves history intact. Export rechecks per-field content rights, formula hazards and disclosure, stages privately and publishes atomically with provenance/codebook/checksums. An evaluation score cannot authorize redistribution.

## Verification

Future proof covers strict attestation/report/model/profile/domain substitutions, expired promotion and revoked rights, product/support dependency denial, deterministic frame/mapping/scoring/empty-denominator negatives and leak-free split handling. Retain actual failures, abstentions, missingness and unsupported strata. Test exact source anchors, candidate-versus-accepted state, old-reader compatibility, no automatic human transition, verifier packet separation and correlated-model labeling.

Use separate assigned-coder API clients for application blinding/late/wrong-actor tests without claiming distinct people. Verify submission/restart/lease replay, conflict/rollback/transaction behavior, immutable alternatives, audit denominators and seeded samples, source drawer row/cell/focus return, bulk preview/partial failures and export rights/formula safety. Real principal/worker and resource claims require their actual separate boundaries. No evaluation, model, dataset, GUI, browser or validator executed for this proposal.
