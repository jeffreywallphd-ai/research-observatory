# GCR-0001 — Restore a generation-safe recovery controller

## Decision request

Authorize one exact, independently reviewed root bootstrap that repairs the recovery controller itself while preserving the sole active `HOLD-W1-GRR-0002`. This is a last-resort control-generation request, not an ordinary ECR, a new GRR hold, an amendment, or Wave execution authority.

## Why this lane is necessary

The repository followed the approved GRR-0002 and ECR-0003 paths through an exact W1.A04 packet approval and a tested four-file B00 candidate. Atomic `taskctl amendment bootstrap-submit` then failed because the released GRR-0001 hold validator is not prefix-stable: when future W1.A04/ECR-0003 is appended in memory, the historical GRR-0001 check includes that later ECR and incorrectly demands ECR-0004.

The mandated same-hold supplement is also unavailable. `recoveryctl supplement-start` accepts only control revision 4, records predecessor revision 4, and advances to the current controller revision 6. The live state is already revision 6, and the v1 supplement boundary additionally requires the target amendment and its bootstrap to exist in the backlog even though this defect prevents that append. No existing approved packet authorizes a direct fix, B00 reopening, competing hold, Wave resume, or manual ledger mutation.

The failed append was atomic. W1 remains `PAUSED`, `CAP-02.S04.T03` remains `BLOCKED`, W1.A04 is absent from the backlog, G1 remains pending, and `HOLD-W1-GRR-0002` remains the sole ACTIVE hold.

## Selected design

Introduce a narrowly bounded Governance Control Recovery (`GCR`) root-of-trust lane for the case where all of the following are proven:

1. an existing ACTIVE GRR hold already denies ordinary execution;
2. the approved repair amendment cannot be appended or enforced because of a recovery-controller defect;
3. the same-hold supplement installer cannot represent the current control generation;
4. no existing approval authorizes the correction; and
5. the proposed repair can remain governance/controller/schema/test-only.

GCR-0001 does not create or replace a hold. Its exact packet, independent packet review, and human exact-commit approval authorize only `GCR-0001.B00`. B00 advances the minimum control revision from 6 to 7 under the existing GRR-0002 hold, adds an append-only control-generation record, and makes the ordinary supplement lane capable of representing `GRR-0002.S01/B01` without inventing W1.A04 backlog state.

The one-time root authority is the repository-owner governance-mismatch protocol: all established executable repair lanes have been proven unable to represent their own next generation, while the existing active hold continues to fail closed. An independently APPROVED packet review therefore permits the repository owner to approve this exact inert packet. That approval is persisted by exclusive creation of `planning/governance-control-recovery/GCR-0001.approval.json` under `governance-control-recovery-runtime.schema.json`. Its introduction commit, on a clean exact `codex/w1-windows-local-runtime` branch, is the sole B00 execution base. It cannot be replaced or edited in place.

The new revision must:

- validate released holds against their historical prefix rather than later amendments;
- parameterize supplement predecessor and successor revisions instead of hard-coding revision 4→6;
- preserve v1 supplement history while adding a versioned pre-append repair target for the approved ECR-0003/W1.A04 B00 boundary;
- deny older readers after revision 7 is recorded;
- require one exact append-only GCR implementation submission, independent control/security review, and no open blocking finding before the new supplement controller is used; and
- retain all existing hold, review, hash, path, stale-writer, dirty-tree, identity, and atomicity controls.

## Non-circular execution and review state machine

1. Freeze this strict-descendant inert packet and preserve `GCR-0001.review-R01.json` append-only. Obtain an independent R02 packet review with every R01 blocker explicitly closed.
2. Only after APPROVED R02 and exact human approval, exclusively create the canonical approval artifact. The commit introducing that artifact is the execution base; B00 may begin only from its clean exact branch HEAD.
3. Implement only `GCR-0001.B00`. The implementation candidate must be a strict descendant of the approval base. `gcrctl submit` compares the complete base-to-candidate changed-path set to the packet allowlist, verifies actor, branch, HEAD, ancestry, hashes, trigger state, active hold, and revision 6, and uses compare-and-swap plus byte-stable failure. The only permitted untracked input is the canonical current-attempt evidence file.
4. Commit the candidate evidence and canonical `GCR-0001.B00.state.json` as the immutable reviewed state. Evidence lives at `artifacts/evidence/governance-control-recovery/GCR-0001.B00.RNN.json`; independent severity-ranked review lives at `planning/governance-control-recovery/GCR-0001.B00.review-RNN.json` and binds the candidate, evidence, and reviewed-state commit.
5. A blocking finding denies approval. The RNN attempt and finding remain append-only; remediation requires a strict-descendant candidate and a fresh canonical RNN evidence record whose schema-bound `findingClosures` exactly reconcile the prior open findings, followed by a new immutable reviewed state. No separate closure-input file exists. Self-review, stale state, hidden paths, changed trigger authority, and in-place replacement fail closed.
6. Live backlog revision remains exactly 6 throughout implementation and review. After an APPROVED B00 ledger is applied to a canonical approved state, commit the pre-adoption evidence. Its direct child may run the single `gcrctl adopt` compare-and-swap transition, changing exactly `planning/backlog.yaml` and the canonical GCR state. That transition appends one generation record binding GCR-0001, its approval, its approved review, predecessor 6, successor 7, actor, and time; activates minimum revision 7; and makes older readers fail closed.
7. Adoption grants no repair-amendment authority. Retain the active GRR-0002 hold and separately prepare, independently review, and obtain exact human approval for `GRR-0002.S01`; install and approve only B01 before retrying the original W1.A04 B00 submission.
8. Continue W1.A04 and W1 only through their existing separate gates.

Canonical commands and artifact identities are frozen in `GCR-0001.packet.json`. The controller is `tools/gcrctl.py`; it defines `submit`, `review`, `resubmit`, `adopt`, and `validate`. The runtime schema governs the approval, evidence, review, state, and adoption records before the controller exists, removing the need to invent a procedure during implementation.

## Safety boundary

GCR-0001 grants no W1.A04 append, amendment materialization, task claim or recovery, T03 mutation, hold release, Wave resume, slice/Wave approval, G1 approval, main integration, remote push, product/reference/privacy change, or review bypass. The untracked failed-submission evidence remains hash-bound trigger evidence only and can never become submitted W1.A04 evidence or authority. Any adverse packet or B00 review remains append-only and stops execution.

## Recommendation

Approve the exact reviewed GCR-0001 packet. Keeping W1 stopped is safe but leaves the repository permanently unable to exercise its prescribed supplement lane at revision 6. Direct edits, reopening an approved bootstrap, faking predecessor revision 4, or creating a competing hold would break the authority chain and are rejected.
