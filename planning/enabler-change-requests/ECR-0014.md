already authorized. One exact owner batch binds the disclosed narrow security
and verification choices; it does not approve a task, Slice, Wave or release.

project recovery. Three new ordered tasks are W2.A06.T01 (L, Core recovery and
Native-owned durable identity), T02 (M, approved Projects integration), and T03
(L, complete quality data and finite verification). These13 planning points are
size estimates, not measured duration or a refactoring budget reset.

current-user and same-machine operational witness, closed45 AST+3 literal
classifier proof, exact current Reader CSP, one unchanged formatter-debt
disposition, complete inventory and bounded independent source/hash recording.

scope, experience or predicates stop. The alternate C18/verification-only plan
leaves product recovery/P1 unmet; retaining frozen methods leaves qualification
blocked. Neither alternative grants a release waiver.

already claimed C18. Actual C18 DONE/approved and quiescence precede metadata-only
B00 submission. B00 proves prepared/authenticated data; installed shell append,
independent B00 review, materialization and activation are later operations.
