## Original candidate: blocking finding

**ECR-0009-preflight-F01 (blocking, delivery circularity).** At `ba45848f`, `W2.A01.T02` was required to prove a live selected-version attachment and real Core/native/disposable-project outcomes before amendment exit. It also excluded new native/Core implementation, while original `CAP-05.S01.T01` was blocked and W2 paused until the amendment was adopted. The installed native code had a `document_runtime::stage_selected_document` helper, but no document command in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/lib.rs`'s Tauri handler; the generic renderer `core_api_request` deliberately denies `/native/document-attachments/{context,stage,candidate,cancel,commit}`. `NativeImportConnection` exposed context and staging, not the remaining candidate/cancel/commit lifecycle. Thus T02 could not honestly meet its real-boundary acceptance, and the original task could not supply the missing bridge before adoption. This was a packet-design blocker, not a test failure to waive.

**ECR-0009-preflight-F02 (security, unsafe stock-drop assumption).** Inspection of the pinned Tauri 2.11.5 drop handling showed that its standard renderer drop event carries dropped absolute paths and grants dropped-file/directory scope. That behavior is incompatible with the approved native-held-source/no-renderer-path boundary for attachment. A renderer drop listener or broad filesystem grant cannot be used as a shortcut. Native-only interception and held-source pinning need later implementation and proof; infeasibility requires a separately approved security redesign.

## Revised boundary inspected in the working tree

The revised [canonical proposal](../../planning/enabler-change-requests/ECR-0009.md), [machine packet](../../planning/enabler-change-requests/ECR-0009.packet.json), and [static review page](../../planning/enabler-change-requests/ECR-0009-review.html) now place reference publication and a **frozen typed opaque renderer command/event contract** in `W2.A01.T02`. Mounted renderer checks use deterministic typed outcomes for selection, association, rights/status, cancellation, retry, errors, focus, and exact-revision handoff. Production choose/drop/attach must remain disabled or explicitly unavailable until the bridge is qualified. T02 may not receive paths or bytes, subscribe to stock Tauri drop-path events, request a broad filesystem grant, or claim mock results as trusted acquisition.

After independent T02 and amendment exit review and supported adoption, the original `CAP-05.S01.T01` must be explicitly resumed through taskctl. It owns fixed-action Tauri commands, native-only picker/drop interception and held-source pinning, completion of the Core stage/candidate/cancel/commit lifecycle, real mounted-to-native/disposable-project safety and rights proofs, and its own independent disposition. Its implementation must satisfy the frozen renderer contract without an unapproved renderer follow-up. `CAP-05.S04` retains protected reader opening. This sequencing removes F01's pre-adoption circularity at the proposal level and addresses F02 by prohibiting the unsafe shortcut; it does **not** prove that native-only interception is feasible or that the real flow works.

The revised rollback separates gates correctly: open gate, reference, renderer-security, opaque-contract, accessibility, or review findings withhold **amendment adoption**; later native/Core trust, drop-path, file-safety, and rights findings keep **the original task and W2 release** blocked. The W2 frozen criteria and separate release gate remain in force. The two additive amendment tasks are M/3 each; proposed supplemental refactoring is 0/143, subject to cumulative accounting during execution.
