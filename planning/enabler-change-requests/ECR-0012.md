# ECR-0012 — protected desktop activation ADR delivery

This inert, append-only proposal corrects one missing architectural delivery
association in approved ECR-0011. It authorizes no implementation until the exact
committed packet is independently reviewed and explicitly approved by the owner.
The frozen W2/A01/A02/A03 packets and completed A03.T01 remain unchanged.

## Problem and evidence

Accepted ADR-0001 requires a changed, indexed, task-linked Proposed or Accepted
ADR in the same protected-path change set. W2.A03.T02 must change the protected
desktop activation and visual baseline, but its frozen seven-file source envelope
contains no ADR or registry path. Proposed ADR-0039 covers T01's controls only.
The read-only planned-path preflight exited 1 for both protected paths before any
consumer edit. Its retained raw output is
`artifacts/tmp/W2.A03.T02.adr-preflight.log`, SHA-256
`a63b91aec33c5f8bc0b6b9592521b5092f8ecd2ba10ee4eb2f8fec54770cb173`.
This is a prospective path check, not product-candidate qualification.

Independent read-only authority review confirmed the mismatch and the supported
retained-base correction sequence. A pre-claim ADR would be unchanged in T02's
real task range; choosing a wider verification base would conceal the missing
association. Bootstrap scope addenda govern B00 only. An unreviewed registry
addition also violates the current v1.4 source-history control.

## Decision and alternatives

**Recommended:** one bounded W2.A04.S01/T01 control/documentary correction while
W2.A03 is paused. Deliver a new Proposed ADR-0040 and its one registry entry in
the retained T02 range, and authenticate only this exact reviewed correction
segment and A03 return/reactivation/reopen chain. Then return to A03 and finish
its already approved consumer task. This satisfies ADR-0001 without weakening
the gate or revising a frozen approval.

**Defer:** keep A03 and W2 paused and T02 blocked. This preserves authority but
leaves the approved 1.8 desktop activation and W2 acquisition outcome incomplete.

**Broader governed replanning:** replace the activation/control approach through
a separately reviewed and approved proposal. This is justified only by new
consequential evidence; the demonstrated issue needs an ADR association and
exact correction attribution, not new product or reference behavior.
