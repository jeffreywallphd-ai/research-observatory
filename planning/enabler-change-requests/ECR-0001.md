## 4. Selected design

### 4.1 ECR classes and interruption

- `operational-clarification`: clarifies approved procedure without changing scope, evidence meaning, independence, or gate legality; does not interrupt a Wave.
- `control-plane-improvement`: changes workflow automation without weakening controls; defaults to the next unapproved Wave and may enter the active Wave only at a stable checkpoint with explicit amendment approval.
- `gate-integrity-safety-defect`: has reproducible proof of a false approval, evidence loss, dependency bypass, destructive transition, or active control failure; pauses affected execution until repaired or explicitly deferred.

Only the last class may interrupt immediately. Classification cannot bypass architecture, experience, benchmark, external-action, or release gates.

### 4.2 Amendment lifecycle

`DRAFT → TRIAGED → DEFERRED | REJECTED | AMENDMENT_PENDING → CHANGES_REQUESTED | APPROVED → MATERIALIZED → ACTIVE → REVIEW → ADOPTED`.

`BLOCKED`, `WITHDRAWN`, and `SUPERSEDED` require rationale and an append-only event. Pending records are non-executable. Only one pending or active amendment is allowed per Wave. Requester, human approver, implementer, and independent reviewer are separately recorded.

Materialization and activation are separate compare-and-swap operations. Activation starts only the bounded amendment campaign; it does not resume ordinary W1 execution. Activation requires a paused Wave at a safe boundary, no ordinary task in `IN_PROGRESS` or `REVIEW`, no frozen candidate mutation, no other active enabler campaign, exact packet and approval blobs, and exact task inventory. `taskctl next` must ignore pending amendments and, while the Wave is paused, must produce a stopped-amendment handoff rather than a Wave start command. After activation, amendment tasks use ordinary evidence, lease, submission, and independent review controls while the ordinary W1 campaign remains paused.

### 4.3 Review workflow

- A submission freezes candidate commit, evidence paths and hashes, selected criteria, changed-path digest, risk rationale, selected checks, and deferred checks.
- One review round returns one consolidated, severity-ranked, reproducible, criterion-bound finding ledger.
- Review attempts are append-only. The current `review` object becomes only a projection over the attempt history.
- Remediation review replays open finding IDs plus the incremental changed-path risk boundary. A material new issue on unchanged scope is recorded as a late-review process defect, while safety/correctness findings remain actionable.
- Recurring or materially expanding findings after round two trigger root-cause escalation rather than unbounded replay.
- Task review remains narrow except for secrets/authentication, security policy, migrations, destructive I/O, evidence/automation controls, public/cross-process contracts, or explicit criteria. Deep adversarial coverage remains at slice review.
- Performance runs occur once, serially, at their declared gate. A failing run is retained rather than retried to green.

### 4.4 Verification selection and telemetry

`verify.py` gains an affected-selection mode that preserves canonical profile definitions and records selected commands, deferred commands, rationale, changed paths, and the later gate that owns deferred coverage. It cannot remove commands from the canonical full profile or change thresholds.

Telemetry records only control metadata: task/amendment ID, review-attempt ID, timestamps, durations, outcome, finding severity/count, selected command IDs, and remediation linkage. It must not record prompts, source content, research data, secrets, paths containing user data, free-form finding bodies, or reviewer chain-of-thought. No historical timing or findings are fabricated.
