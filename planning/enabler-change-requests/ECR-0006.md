# ECR-0006 — Align CAP-03.S06 revisitable-cycle authority

Status: pending independent packet review and explicit exact-commit human
approval; non-executable.

Proposed authority: `W1.A07`, ordered after adopted `W1.A01`–`W1.A03`, the
terminal `W1.A04` reservation superseded by `GOV-MIG-0001`, and adopted
`W1.A05`–`W1.A06`.

Classification: the conservative v4
`product-scope-security-experience` envelope. This packet changes one
product-scope planning sentence only. It does not change runtime behavior,
security authority, or the governed experience; it aligns the task with
accepted ADR-0026 and the already approved `RO-UI-ACADEMIC-MINIMAL-1.5`
workflow catalog.

## 1. Decision requested

Approve one append-only W1 amendment with one CAP-03 contribution and one
small task:

- `W1.A07.S01 / W1.A07.T01` replaces the isolated
  `CAP-03.S06.T05` acceptance-criterion term `critical` with
  `manuscript-review/revision` in the canonical backlog and approved slice
  plan, then regenerates derived planning views.

The exact replacement is:

> Hermeneutic, manuscript-review/revision, and living-review cycles can revisit
> earlier steps without rewriting history; systematic review maintains its
> reproducible order and audit endpoint.

No production code, runtime behavior, schema, migration, interface, or visual
design changes in this amendment. After independent completion and adoption,
ordinary W1 may resume and recover `CAP-03.S06.T05` under the corrected
authority.

## 2. Evidence for the conflict

The current lower-authority T05 criterion says hermeneutic, critical, and
living-review cycles are revisitable. Accepted ADR-0026 requires the runtime
contract to project the exact approved Academic Minimal 1.5 workflow catalog.
Those exact catalog bytes classify `living-review`, `hermeneutic-inquiry`, and
`manuscript-review-revision` as revisitable, while
`critical-problematization` and `critical-article-development` are linear.

The T05 criterion therefore cannot be implemented literally without violating
accepted architecture and the approved experience. T05 was stopped before any
product or test implementation, at a committed and recoverable boundary.

## 3. Selected resolution

1. Preserve the exact approved catalog cycle policy.
2. Replace only `critical` with `manuscript-review/revision` in the two
   authoritative T05 planning locations.
3. Preserve the rest of the criterion, all other CAP-03.S06 authority, task
   state, dependencies, estimates, verification duties, and W1 obligations.
4. Keep product and governed-experience bytes unchanged.

This is an authority correction, not refactoring. The refactor allocation is
zero of the immutable 194-point W1 baseline.

1. apply the exact sentence replacement to both authoritative locations;

3. prove no other CAP-03.S06 semantic authority or product path changes;
4. validate the canonical backlog and generated review views; and

## 5. Alternatives

1. **Align T05 to ADR-0026 and the approved catalog — selected.** This is the
   smallest coherent correction and preserves existing product and experience
   authority.
2. **Make critical profiles revisitable.** Rejected because it would contradict
   accepted architecture and change the governed workflow experience.
3. **Make manuscript review linear.** Rejected because it would discard the
   approved review/revision loop and contradict current contracts.
4. **Defer T05 or CAP-03.S06.** Available but not recommended; it would leave W1
   incomplete despite a one-word planning defect with an unambiguous
   higher-authority answer.
